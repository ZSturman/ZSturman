[![Github Repo](https://img.shields.io/badge/Github%20Repo-181717?style=flat-square&logo=github&logoColor=white)](https://github.com/ZSturman/TopNote) [![App Store](https://img.shields.io/badge/App%20Store-000000?style=flat-square&logo=apple&logoColor=white)](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/top-note/id6742725271)

> A widget-first productivity app that keeps you focused on what's next and only on what’s next.

<details>
<summary>More details</summary>

> Top Note is a focused productivity app for people who tend to ignore reminders or feel weighed down by long to-do lists. It takes a simpler approach: one reminder, flashcard, or note at a time. You can complete it or skip it, and if you skip, it returns sooner through a spaced repetition system that keeps important things visible. This design encourages accountability and steady progress. It helps build habits, strengthen memory, and keep personal ideas within reach—without the noise of endless lists. Why it works Top Note combines two familiar ideas: single-task focus and spaced repetition. Together they keep your attention on what matters next, reinforcing memory and consistency over time. Features – One-at-a-time reminders that reduce distraction. – Skip and repeat so skipped items return sooner. – Spaced repetition flashcards for better recall. – Widget-first design that keeps the app close at hand. – Flexible note support for quotes, goals, or brief thoughts. – Simple setup inside the app; most use happens in the widget. Best for – People who often dismiss reminders. – Habit tracking and reinforcement. – Short study or review sessions. – Keeping a meaningful note visible through the day. Top Note makes productivity manageable by narrowing your focus. Instead of managing a list, you engage with one small task—and let time handle the rest.

### [**ChewSense**](https://github.com/ZSturman/ChewSense---Data-Collection-and-Labeling) <sub><sup style="color:#999">Active · Development</sup></sub>

[![Repo](https://img.shields.io/badge/Repo-181717?style=flat-square&logo=github&logoColor=white)](https://github.com/ZSturman/ChewSense---Data-Collection-and-Labeling)

> An iOS app that uses AirPods’ motion sensors to detect chewing and help users eat more mindfully by slowing down their pace.

<details>
<summary>More details</summary>

> ChewSense is an iOS app that helps people slow down while they eat and pay more attention to their habits. It uses the motion sensors in AirPods to pick up chewing—mainly through X-axis acceleration—and gives gentle alerts when someone might be eating too fast. The app records motion and optional video so it can learn a person’s eating pattern and adjust over time. The goal is simple: improve digestion, avoid overeating, and make meals feel more mindful without asking the user to track anything manually.

</details>

### [**Episodic Memory Agent**](https://github.com/ZSturman/Episodic-Memory-Agent) <sub><sup style="color:#999">Active · Development</sup></sub>

[![Repo](https://img.shields.io/badge/Repo-181717?style=flat-square&logo=github&logoColor=white)](https://github.com/ZSturman/Episodic-Memory-Agent)

> A modular cognitive agent framework that models how experience becomes memory.

<details>
<summary>More details</summary>

> An event-segmented episodic memory agent designed to simulate how an intelligent system perceives, interprets, and organizes experience over time. The architecture centers on a strict cognitive loop—resolving location, identifying entities, detecting change—and freezing those moments into structured episodes. Built with swappable modules, strict invariants, and full logging, the system prioritizes learning over hardcoded assumptions, user-defined semantics over predefined categories, and reproducible behavior over opaque heuristics.

</details>
